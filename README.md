## 🧩 WHAT IS THIS?

Marine pollution **never stays still**. It rides currents, dances with the wind, and drifts with the tides — so chasing it with a net and a prayer doesn't work.

**This platform flips the script:** instead of *reacting* to pollution, it *predicts* it.

We fuse **satellite imagery**, **ocean-current data**, **wind conditions**, **weather intelligence**, and **historical pollution observations** into one AI brain that:

| 🎯 It finds | 🔮 It predicts | 🚤 It optimizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where floating plastic & debris accumulate **right now** | Where that waste will drift **next** | Which hotspot to clean **first** |
| Concentrated pollution zones on an **interactive map** | Future accumulation zones before they form | Which **route** to take to get there |
| Hidden hotspots satellites can't show humans at a glance | Movement trajectories from currents + wind | How to split **boats, fuel & crew** for max impact |

**The result?** A reactive cleanup chore becomes an intelligent, surgical, data-driven intervention. 🌍
